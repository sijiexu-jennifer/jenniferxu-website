# Submission checklist

Before submitting, confirm the author name in `index.qmd` and render the
webpage one final time.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Push this repository to GitHub.
2. Open **Settings > Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select the `main` branch and `/docs` folder, then save.
5. Open the published URL and confirm that all four figures load.

Submit both the published blog URL and the GitHub repository URL.

## Rubric cross-check

- The introduction states a substantive geographic question and motivation.
- GIS methods and rate transformations are explained for a first-time reader.
- Every visual is interpreted and the article ends with a clear takeaway.
- Official Census sources, units, projection, and map notes are documented.
- The repository has a clear structure and exact replication instructions.
- Downloads, transformations, joins, maps, and webpage are generated by code.
- Appropriate county-level population and geographic data are used.
- Two substantive county maps are central to the analysis.
- Maps use a legible equal-area projection and meaningful diverging scales.
- The article explains the economic meaning and limitations of spatial
  patterns instead of merely describing colors.
